# Artwork provenance

Created for Touch Grass on 2 October 2026 using the built-in image-generation tool. These are original editorial assets, not user discoveries or validated botanical identification plates. Optimized WebP files are committed; the high-resolution generated originals remain in the local generation archive.

## Woodland illustration

Prompt direction: a sunlit woodland with an old oak, slender trees, a winding footpath, delicate ferns and small yellow wildflowers. Gouache and colored pencil on warm ivory, muted moss and olive. Intimate natural-history-book illustration. No people, text, UI, or logos. 1200px WebP.

## Oak study

Prompt direction: a complete oak twig with five lobed leaves and two acorns. Fine pencil veins and gouache washes, muted moss, sage undersides, ochre caps. Isolated specimen with a transparent background, no labels or shadow. 600px WebP with alpha.

## Fern study

Prompt direction: a curved fern frond and smaller curled fiddlehead. Fine pencil, translucent watercolor edges, muted olive and sage. Complete specimen on transparency, no background, text, or shadow. 600px WebP with alpha.

## Existing photographs

`canopy.webp`, `forest.webp`, and `woodland.webp` are the original optimized Unsplash editorial photographs; their source links remain in the root README. They are used in reading guides and integration smoke checks.
